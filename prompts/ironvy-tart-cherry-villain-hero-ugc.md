## 4. Voice and delivery
- Villain: dry, slightly smug British male voice, medium pace.
- Hero: warm, calm, confident British female or male voice, about 150 wpm.
- It should feel like a mini story, not a sales pitch.
- Music: tense low drone in act 1 → warm acoustic bed in act 2 (-20 dB).

## 5. Captions
Burned-in captions throughout: bold white sans-serif with a soft shadow, 2–4 words per line, centred in the lower third and inside the safe zone (clear of the platform UI). Key numbers in red (#C8102E).

## 6. Compliance notes (UK)
- **Don't** mention a medicine (allopurinol, ibuprofen) and don't say it replaces one.
- **Don't** say "gout", "arthritis", "inflammation", "uric acid", "cures", "stops flares" or "clears crystals". Tart cherry has **no authorised health claims** in GB.
- Comparisons with juice are fine, but **only factual** (sugar content per serving). Juice sugar figure: check the source before you go live.
