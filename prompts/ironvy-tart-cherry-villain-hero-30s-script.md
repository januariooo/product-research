> I'm uric acid. You cut out the beer. The steak. The shellfish. And I'm still here.
> Your diet only stops new uric acid coming in. It doesn't deal with what's already built up. So I keep coming back.
>
> **[HERO – warm, confident]**
> Hi. I'm tart cherry.
> Packed with anthocyanins from Montmorency cherries, I help maintain healthy uric acid levels and support your joints.
> 3,600 milligrams per serving. No sugar like cherry juice. Just two capsules a day.
> Ironvy Tart Cherry. Tap below before this batch sells out.

## Production notes
- **Two voices:** villain = low British male voice, smug; hero = warm British voice (male or female).
- **Colours:** act 1 (0–11 s) blue-grey and dark; act 2 (11–30 s) warm golden morning light.
- **Music:** low, tense drone → warm acoustic at the transition (-20 dB).
- **Captions:** bold white with a soft shadow, 2–4 words per line, lower third; claims with a red ✓ (#C8102E).
- **Product:** use the Ironvy product photo as the reference so the label is correct.
