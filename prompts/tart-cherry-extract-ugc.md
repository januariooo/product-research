## 1. Master video-generation prompt (copy-paste)

> Vertical 9:16 handheld smartphone UGC video, 25 seconds. A friendly, natural-looking woman in her late 40s with light smile lines, minimal makeup, hair loosely tied back, wearing a soft grey running zip-up. She is in a cozy home kitchen in the early morning, filmed by warm golden window light from the side, with soft amber tones and gentle shadows. A small amount of natural handheld camera shake, filmed at eye level as if self-recorded on a phone propped on the counter. She speaks directly to the camera in a calm, genuine, conversational tone, like sharing a tip with a friend. On the counter: a dark glass bottle of tart cherry extract with a clean label, a glass of water, a pair of running shoes in the background, and a small bowl of fresh deep-red sour cherries. Realistic skin texture, no beauty filter, shallow depth of field, authentic and unpolished, not an advert. 4K, natural colour grade, warm highlights.

**Negative prompt:** studio lighting, glossy commercial look, heavy makeup, airbrushed skin, young model, text artifacts, distorted hands, extra fingers, warped label, exaggerated facial expressions, cold blue tones.

## 2. Script and shot list (~25 s)

**Cut to 15 s:** keep the 0–3 s hook, cut 3–8 s, shorten the reveal to 8–12 s and the pour to 12–14 s, then end on the CTA.

## 4. Voice and delivery
- Warm, mid-pitch, unhurried pace (about 140 wpm) with natural pauses and a light laugh allowed.
- It should sound like a peer recommendation, not a sales pitch.
- Optional music: soft acoustic or lo-fi bed at -20 dB under the voice.

## 5. Captions
Burned-in captions throughout: bold white sans-serif with a soft shadow, 2–4 words per line, centred in the lower third and inside the safe zone (clear of the platform UI).

## 6. Compliance notes
- Keep it to experience-based language ("I feel…", "part of my routine").
- **Don't** claim it treats, cures or prevents anything (e.g., inflammation, arthritis, gout or insomnia).
- If the claims are paid or sponsored, add "#ad" / "Paid partnership" as the platform requires.
- If the copy mentions sleep or recovery, consider adding a small note that it's a food supplement and results vary.
